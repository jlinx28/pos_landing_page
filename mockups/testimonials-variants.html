Pick a Testimonials direction

Same reviews, four different presentations.

V1 · Spotlight + Stats

One hero quote + stats panel

Big featured testimonial in a white card with hero stats sidebar (1,200+ stores · 4.9★ · ₱42M monthly). Then a 3-up strip of secondary quotes below.

✅ Strong trust-building (stats prominent)
✅ Best for converting skeptics
⚠️ Featured story dominates
V2 · Marquee

Two rows auto-scrolling

10+ testimonials in two infinite-scrolling rows (top: cream, bottom: dark cocoa, opposite directions). Edge fades. Bottom 4.9 / 5 · 1,200+ owners pill.

✅ Most kinetic / alive
✅ Conveys "many testimonials" instantly
⚠️ Quotes hard to read while moving
V3 · Polaroid Wall

Scattered tilted polaroids

6 polaroid-style cards scattered at various rotations, each with a colored "photo" (gradient + emoji + pattern) and handwritten-style Caveat font quote. Push pin decoration on top. Hover lifts card.

✅ Most charming / brand-defining
✅ Warm, hand-crafted feel
⚠️ Hand font readability varies
V4 · Industry-tagged

Each quote labeled by industry

1 featured big card with industry pill (🏪 Sari-sari) + 3-stat sidebar (3 yrs / +38% / 0 lost sales). Plus 6 smaller cards each tagged by industry (🍜 ☕ 💈 💊 👗 🥖). Connects to your Industries section.

✅ Reinforces "fits every business"
✅ Prospects find their type fast
⚠️ Most structured, least playful

My pick: V3 (Polaroid Wall) — most original and on-brand for the warm Filipino feel; nobody's POS site does this. V4 (Industry-tagged) if you want it to reinforce the Industries section above. V1 (Spotlight) if conversion through trust signals is the priority.
