Pick a sticky direction

All in the V6 sticky-note family — different paper styles.

N1 · Notebook

Torn-out lined notebook pages

Off-white paper with horizontal rule lines and a vertical red margin line. Three "spiral binding" holes punched on the left edge. Handwritten Caveat font for question titles. Slight rotations.

✅ Most "owner's notebook" warmth
✅ Handwritten feel without being illegible
⚠️ Caveat font is informal
N2 · Washi-Tape

White notes "taped" with washi strips

Each white note has a single washi tape strip at the top in palette colors (saffron / forest / burnt) at small angles. Striped diagonal pattern within the tape. Pinterest aesthetic.

✅ Crafty / aesthetic
✅ Less rotation = easier to read
⚠️ Reads as "lifestyle blog"
N3 · Postcards

Vintage postcards with stamp + divider

Each card is a postcard (2:1 horizontal): question on left, answer on right, divider line down the middle. Stamp area in top-right with a relevant emoji. "FAQ #01" mono tag in top-left, "RetailFlow · Mfla" return address bottom-left.

✅ Most distinctive layout
✅ Vintage / collectible feel
⚠️ Horizontal cards = different rhythm
N4 · Thread Wall

Notes connected by red yarn to a hub

6 sticky notes scattered around the edges, all connected by red yarn (SVG curves) to a centered "Got another question?" hub card. Detective/conspiracy-board vibe — every Q points back to "talk to us."

✅ Reinforces "contact us first" model
✅ Strong central CTA
⚠️ Heavy section, mobile-tricky

My pick: N1 (Notebook) — most "owner's notebook" warmth and the lined paper looks distinctive without going full-craft. N4 (Thread Wall) if you want to lean into the "contact us" model. N3 (Postcards) for the most distinctive layout.
