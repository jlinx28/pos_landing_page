Pick a Story Rows variation

Same alternating-rows DNA, three different visual treatments.

V2 · Wrapped Cards

Each row in its own card

Rows are visually contained — soft tinted gradients, clean small "01 / POINT OF SALE" labels in the corner. Tighter, more product-page feel.

✅ Strong containment — easy to scan
✅ Each feature feels like a deliberate beat
⚠️ Less dramatic than full-bleed
V3 · Editorial Magazine

Display numerals + dot-pattern halos

Huge "01 / 02 / 03" numerals sit behind each headline as design elements. More breathing room, magazine-page feel. Asymmetric and confident.

✅ Most distinctive / brand-forward
✅ Headlines feel like article titles
⚠️ More vertical space per row
V4 · Full-bleed Bands

Each feature gets its own colored section

Cream → dark cocoa → white → cream-deep. Each band is edge-to-edge with strong color shifts. Apple-product-page energy. Bonus: a final tile strip catches the secondary features.

✅ Highest visual rhythm
✅ Dark band gives the page contrast
⚠️ Heaviest section — long page

My pick: V4 (Full-bleed Bands) — the dark cocoa middle band breaks up the cream-heavy rest of the page and makes the offline message land hard. The "plus everything else" tile strip at the end mops up the remaining features without bloating the marquee rows. V3 is the most stylish if you want the page to feel like a thoughtful brand statement.

Reply V2, V3, or V4 — or mix ("V4's bands but use V3's big numerals inside them").
