Pick your hero visual

Same "copy + chips on the left, big visual on the right" structure — four different visual treatments.

V1 · Phone Hub

Phone-centered, industries orbit

Big phone in the center showing the POS app. 6 industry chip-cards float around it with dashed connecting lines. Concentric backdrop circles. Product is the hero.

✅ Product-forward (phone is the hero)
✅ Floating cards animate gently
⚠️ Less "place" feeling than streetscape
V2 · Isometric Town

3D bird's-eye street block

Same idea as the original D but in isometric 3D — buildings have depth, you can see roof peaks, side walls. POS terminal floats overhead with dashed signal lines. "✓ All synced" badge.

✅ Most modern / dimensional
✅ Strong "town" sense
⚠️ Iso style is harder to extend
V3 · Counter Close-up

In-the-moment shop counter

Inside a wooden-counter scene with shelves of bottles, a hanging "OPEN" sign, scattered peso bills, receipt paper, and a coffee cup. The phone sits on the counter showing the POS. Most "in-use" feeling.

✅ Most evocative / cinematic
✅ Shows product in context
⚠️ Single-scene = less "many industries" feel
V4 · Stacked Storefronts

Poster / fanned playing-card layout

4 storefront illustration cards arranged in a fan with rotation, stacked at different depths. "⭐ Most popular" Sari-sari card sits in the front with the saffron border. Phone mockup at the bottom-center, on top of all cards. Editorial poster feel.

✅ Most poster-like / brand-forward
✅ Hierarchy: featured card stands out
⚠️ Storefront cards smaller than full SVG illos

My pick: V2 (Isometric Town) — same conceptual win as the original D but with more depth and modernity. The 3D buildings + floating POS terminal feels like a real "system at work." V3 (Counter) if you want the page to feel more cinematic. V1 (Phone Hub) if you want the product to be the hero, not the storefronts.
