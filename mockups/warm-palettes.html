Pick a palette

All three keep the warm/local soul. Differences are in temperature and personality.

V1 · Terracotta

Earthy, artisan

Like sun-baked clay and adobe walls. Refined and grounded — closer to a thoughtful brand than a budget tool.

✅ Refined, premium feel
✅ Distinctive in POS category
⚠️ Slightly more "lifestyle brand" energy
V2 · Coral

Friendly, golden-hour

Soft, approachable, hospitality-adjacent. Reads as restaurant/café-friendly first.

✅ Warmest of the three
✅ Strong appeal to F&B owners
⚠️ Could feel less serious for retail/inventory crowd
V3 · Saffron

Spice market, food-y

Saffron + forest green = local market vibes. Energetic but grounded by deep cocoa text and forest accent.

✅ Most "Filipino market" feel
✅ Two-tone palette = more visual range
⚠️ Saffron orange close to current accent — less fresh

My pick: V1 (Terracotta) — it's the most differentiated. POS competitors all look like dashboards (blues, greens, grays); a terracotta-and-clay palette with a warm serif headline reads as a real brand, not a SaaS template. Looks equally good for a restaurant, a sari-sari, or a salon.
