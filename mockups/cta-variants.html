Pick a CTA direction

All four capture leads. Different friction levels and visual treatments.

V1 · Inline Pill

Single email + button

One pill-shaped container with email input + "Send my trial link" button. Trust line below. Most common SaaS pattern.

✅ Highest conversion (1 field)
✅ Cleanest visual
⚠️ No segmentation data
V2 · Split Form

Copy + benefits left, form right

Left: heading + 4-bullet benefits list. Right: white card form with name + email + business type dropdown. Most "designed" feel.

✅ Best for qualified leads
✅ Reinforces benefits at form moment
⚠️ 3 fields = more friction
V3 · Card Form

Centered hero card with chip selector

Centered white card with "14 days free" ribbon. Email field + 4-button business-type chip selector (Sari-sari · Restaurant · Café · Other). Tap-friendly.

✅ Most tap-friendly (no dropdowns)
✅ Visual + emoji-driven
⚠️ Limits to 3-4 visible business types
V4 · Conversational

Messenger-thread email capture

Bot intro bubble ("Tara! Drop your email…") + email input + business-type chips. Perfectly on-brand with your How-It-Works chat motif.

✅ Cohesive with chat-first brand voice
✅ Filipino Messenger-culture friendly
✅ Lower perceived friction

My pick: V4 (Conversational) — directly extends the Messenger-thread motif from your How-It-Works section, so the page feels like one cohesive story ("we talk to you, you give us your email, we send the link"). V3 (Card Form) for the most polished standard CTA. V1 (Inline) if maximum conversion matters most.
