FAQ

Common questions.

Answers to the things owners ask us first.

Does it really work without internet? ⌄

Yes. All transactions, inventory, and reports are stored locally via SQLite. Internet is only needed for license activation.

What devices does it run on? ⌄

Any Android phone or tablet running Android 8 or newer. Cheap tablets work fine.

Can I use my barcode scanner & printer? ⌄

Yes. USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners + most ESC/POS thermal printers (58mm and 80mm).

How do I pay? ⌄

GCash, Maya, or bank transfer. Pay monthly or buy a lifetime license.

What happens if I cancel? ⌄

Your data stays on your device. Export everything to CSV anytime. No lock-in.

Is my data safe? ⌄

Yes. Data lives on your device — we never see it. Optional encrypted backups to USB or LAN.

Do you offer training & support? ⌄

Email and Messenger support on all plans. Pro and Custom get priority support.

Can I run multiple cashiers? ⌄

Yes — Pro and Custom plans support multi-device LAN sync. Up to 3 (Pro) or unlimited (Custom).

Pick a FAQ direction

Same questions, four different ways to surface them.

V1 · Two-column

Compact side-by-side accordion

Same accordion as today, but split into 2 columns. Cuts vertical scroll roughly in half. Most utilitarian.

✅ Shortest section
✅ Familiar pattern
⚠️ Stacks on mobile (no real change there)
V2 · Categorized Tabs

Filter by topic, then accordion

5 category chips (Setup · Pricing · Offline · Security · Support) + accordion below. "Showing 4 of 24 questions" hint scales when you grow the FAQ.

✅ Best for many FAQs
✅ Owners self-route to their topic
⚠️ Hidden content = lower SEO
V3 · Chat

Like a real Messenger thread

Q's as user bubbles (right, saffron), A's as RetailFlow Support bubbles (left, white). Includes inline payment grid in the GCash answer + a fake "Ask your own question…" input + Send CTA. Ties to your "contact-us-first" model.

✅ Cohesive with How-It-Works tone
✅ Filipino Messenger-culture friendly
⚠️ Heavier visual load per Q&A
V4 · Help-center

Search bar + sidebar nav + main column

Big search bar (with "Popular: brownout · multi-device · printer" chips) + sticky sidebar with 6 categories and counts + main accordion column with numbered Q's, sort dropdown, and 👍/👎 helpful buttons. Most "documentation site" feel.

✅ Scales to 50+ questions
✅ Most "serious software" credibility
⚠️ Heaviest section, may feel overkill for <10 Qs

My pick: V1 (Two-column) — clean, scannable, keeps all answers immediately visible (best SEO). V3 (Chat) if you want to lean into the "talk to us" brand voice. V2 (Tabs) if your FAQ list grows past ~15 questions.
