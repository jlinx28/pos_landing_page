Side-by-side comparison

Quick read on the trade-offs of each features layout.

Variant A

Tabbed Showcase

One feature in focus, others a click away. Premium, app-like feel.

✅ Compact — saves vertical space
✅ Feels interactive & modern
⚠️ Requires JS for tab switching
⚠️ Hidden content = lower SEO weight
Variant B

Story Rows

Each marquee feature gets a full row with imagery. Immersive, narrative.

✅ High visual impact
✅ Each feature gets full attention
⚠️ Long page — lots of scrolling
⚠️ Best for 4–6 features, not 10
Variant C

Uniform Icon Grid

Equal-weight cards in a 3-column grid. Clean, no surprises.

✅ Best for SEO (all content visible, crawlable)
✅ Easy to scan
✅ Handles 9–12 features comfortably
⚠️ Less visual hierarchy / drama
Variant D

Magazine / Editorial

Hero feature spotlight, two medium cards, strip of secondary chips. Editorial weight.

✅ Strong visual hierarchy
✅ Showcases your "killer feature"
✅ Works for many features without bloat
⚠️ Slightly more design effort to maintain

My pick: Variant D (Magazine) — strongest hierarchy, gets your "offline POS" superpower top billing, and still surfaces everything else without becoming a wall of cards. Add semantic markup and it's SEO-equivalent to Variant C. If you want pure scannability and zero JS, go C.
