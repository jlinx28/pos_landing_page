Round 2 — pick a How It Works

V4+ is the enhanced journey. V5/V6/V7 are fresh new takes.

V4+ · Journey Enhanced

Curved path with detail cards

Same playful path layout, now each step is a full card with: icon + number + time tag (Day 0 / <1hr / Day 0–14 / Day 14) + description + (step 4) payment chips. Bottom summary callout shows total setup time.

✅ Keeps the playful journey feel
✅ Now has real depth per step
✅ Time tags = transparency
V5 · Phone Notifications

Notifications drop on a phone screen

Left: 4 step cards with description. Right: a phone showing 5 notifications dropping in sequence (with animation). Each notif is a real-feeling Messenger / system notification with timestamp. Most product-y.

✅ Animated — feels alive
✅ Reinforces the product (it's an app)
⚠️ Animation may distract
V6 · Receipt

Looks like a printed POS receipt

A real-feeling receipt with serrated edges, dashed dividers, mono font, "[✓]" checkmarks per step, totals at the bottom (Active time / Calendar time / Trial cost / After trial), and a "★★★★★ SALAMAT · COME AGAIN!" footer with a fake barcode. Most thematic — your product literally prints these.

✅ Most on-brand (you make POS!)
✅ Memorable and unique
⚠️ Slimmer / less hero-image energy
V7 · Day Timeline

Calendar-based horizontal timeline

Top: a horizontal day strip (Day 0 → Day 14) with marker dots. Below: 3 grouped cards (Day 0 has steps 1+2, Day 0–14 has step 3, Day 14 has steps 4+5). Bottom: 3-column summary (5 steps · ~15 min · 14 days). Most informational.

✅ Conveys "low time commitment" hard
✅ Shows trial duration visually
⚠️ Most structured, least playful

My pick: V6 (Receipt) — your product is a POS that literally prints these. Using a receipt as the metaphor for the setup steps is the most on-brand and memorable thing on the whole page. V4+ (Journey Enhanced) if you want to keep the playful path feel with proper depth.
