FAQ

Owner's notebook.

Quick answers to the things owners ask first.

Q1 · Does it work without internet?

Yes — all data stored locally with SQLite. Brownouts can't stop you. Sync happens when you reconnect.

Q2 · What devices?

Any Android 8+ phone or tablet. Cheap tablets are fine.

Q3 · Scanner & printer?

USB & Bluetooth scanners + ESC/POS thermal printers (58mm/80mm).

Q4 · How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, or bank transfer. Monthly or lifetime.

GCash Maya Bank

Q5 · What if I cancel?

Data stays on your device. Export to CSV anytime. No lock-in.

Q6 · Is my data safe?

Lives only on your device. Encrypted backups to USB or LAN. No cloud breach risk.

Q7 · Training & support?

Email + Messenger on all plans. Pro/Custom get priority support.
