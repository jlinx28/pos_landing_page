Side-by-side comparison

Quick read on the trade-offs of each direction.

Direction A

Clean & Premium

Reads as enterprise-grade software. Wins trust from owners comparing to bigger tools.

✅ Builds credibility
✅ Easy to scan & convert
⚠️ Could feel cold / generic
⚠️ Less local personality
Direction B

Bold & Confident

High-energy. Stands out in a category full of beige B2B sites.

✅ Memorable, distinctive
✅ Strong CTAs that demand action
⚠️ Polarizing — not everyone loves it
⚠️ Risk of feeling shouty
Direction C

Filipino Warmth

"Made for us, by us" feel. Strong differentiator vs. global SaaS competitors.

✅ Deep local connection
✅ Bilingual SEO bonus (Tagalog terms)
⚠️ Can feel "small" to enterprise buyers
⚠️ Tagalog copy needs careful proofreading
Direction D

Polished Dark

Same DNA as today, just sharper. Lower-risk evolution of what already works.

✅ Familiar to existing visitors
✅ Tech-forward, modern
⚠️ Less of a "wow, that's new" moment
⚠️ Dark mode reads as developer-tool, not retail

My pick: Direction C (Filipino Warmth) is the strongest differentiator — no global competitor can copy it. Combine it with the structural rigor of A (clean hierarchy, generous whitespace) and you get a site that feels both local and credible. Whichever you pick, all four would ship with the same SEO foundation: semantic HTML, JSON-LD schema, sitemap/robots, prerendering, FAQ section, OG/Twitter cards.
