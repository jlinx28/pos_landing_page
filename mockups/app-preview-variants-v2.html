Round 2 — pick a direction

Building on what you liked about V2 and V3.

V5 · Hybrid

Coverflow + side annotations

Literal merge of V2 and V3 — coverflow center spotlight with neighboring phones, plus 6 numbered annotation cards on the sides. Pagination + screen-name strip below.

✅ Best of both worlds
✅ Most informationally dense
⚠️ Busiest section — lots happening
V6 · Pop-outs

UI elements extracted as floating cards

Big phone center, 6 floating "pop-out" cards around it — each shows a real UI element (search bar, category chips, cart button, product card, bottom nav, offline indicator) with description. Dashed connector lines.

✅ Most product-detail-focused
✅ Shows actual UI in close-up
⚠️ Densest visual — needs space
V7 · Orbital

Phone hub, 8 screens orbit around

POS phone in the center as the "hub," 8 small screens orbit around it on concentric dashed rings. Curved dashed connectors from center to each. Most diagrammatic / brand-forward.

✅ Most visually distinctive
✅ Conveys "all-in-one" at a glance
⚠️ Needs square space — mobile-tricky
V8 · Triptych

3 phones, 3 moments in a day

"8AM Opening · 1PM Lunch rush · 9PM Closing" — three big phones in a row, each with a story title and screen description. Phones rise on a step (translateY) for visual rhythm. Most narrative.

✅ Tells a story (a full day)
✅ Each phone has clear context
⚠️ Only 3 screens shown (rest in footer note)

My pick: V6 (Pop-outs) — most original; competitors use carousels and grids, nobody extracts UI elements as floating annotations like this. V7 (Orbital) if you want maximum visual distinctiveness. V8 (Triptych) if you want this section to tell a narrative.
