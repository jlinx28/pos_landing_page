Pick a How It Works direction

All four show the new 5-step flow: Contact → We send link → Try free → Pay → Activate.

V1 · Stepper

5 cards in a row, dashed arrows between

Closest to your current design — same numbered icon cards, just expanded to 5 steps with dashed-arrow connectors. Step 5 is highlighted in saffron as the "finish."

✅ Familiar pattern
✅ Lowest disruption
⚠️ Tight at 5 cols on mid screens
V2 · Chat

Messenger-style conversation

Renders the actual flow as a real chat: customer messages on right (saffron), RetailFlow on left (white). Includes a fake input + "Start chat" CTA at the bottom. Most distinctive.

✅ Instantly conveys "talk to us first"
✅ Filipino-friendly (Messenger culture)
⚠️ Less of a step-list feel
V3 · Timeline

Vertical stacked with connecting dashes

Numbered circles down the left, content cards on the right. Connecting dashed line. Step 5 has the saffron "finish" treatment with payment chips inside Step 4. Most readable.

✅ Most scannable on mobile
✅ Each step gets full breathing room
⚠️ Vertical = longer scroll
V4 · Journey

Steps along a curved dashed path

Steps placed along a winding path SVG, like a board game. Step 5 is the "FINISH" with a flag tag. Most playful + memorable.

✅ Most distinctive / playful
✅ Implies progress / journey
⚠️ Mobile collapses (path needs width)

My pick: V2 (Chat) — your "contact us first" model is the central differentiator; showing it as a real Messenger thread makes the unusual flow feel natural and inviting (especially for the Messenger-heavy Filipino market). V3 (Timeline) if you want the most traditional/scannable presentation. V4 (Journey) if you want the most visually distinctive treatment.
