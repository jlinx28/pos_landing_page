Pick an App Preview variant

All four show the same screenshots — different ways of presenting them.

V1 · Bento Mosaic

Hero phone + mixed cards

POS phone gets a 5-col × 4-row hero card with "Most-used screen" badge. Other screens fill smaller spots with image-cover cards. Asymmetric, varied hierarchy.

✅ Strong visual hierarchy
✅ Featured screen gets star treatment
⚠️ Many image-cover cards = darker overall
V2 · Coverflow

Center spotlight, sides fading

5 phones in a row — center one big & glowing, neighbors at 0.82 scale, far ones at 0.65 + 25% opacity. Pagination dots + clickable screen-name strip below.

✅ Most kinetic / dynamic
✅ Familiar carousel UX
⚠️ Needs JS for full interactivity
V3 · Annotated

One phone with 6 feature callouts

Big POS phone center, 3 numbered annotations on each side connected by dashed curves. Strip of other 5 screens below at 60% opacity. Most explanatory.

✅ Best for explaining the UX
✅ Tells a story about design quality
⚠️ Heavy on copy — adds vertical space
V4 · Editorial Wall

Phones scattered, light bg

7 phones at varying angles, sizes, depths — like a designer's pinboard. Big POS center with glow, 6 satellites with rotation. Light cream background instead of dark — only variant that breaks the dark.

✅ Most editorial / brand-forward
✅ Phones float with gentle animation
⚠️ Light bg breaks dark-section page rhythm

My pick: V1 (Bento Mosaic) — strongest hierarchy and keeps the dark band that gives your page rhythm. V3 (Annotated) if you want this section to teach more about the product. V4 (Wall) is the most distinctive but breaks the page's dark/light alternation.
