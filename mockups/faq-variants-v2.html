— Frequently asked

Everything you'd want to ask first.

01

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. All transactions, inventory updates, and reports are stored locally on the device using SQLite. Internet is only needed for license activation and (optionally) cloud backups. Multi-device sync uses your local WiFi — no cloud required.

02

What devices does it run on?

Any Android phone or tablet running Android 8 or newer. Most cheap tablets work fine — you don't need expensive hardware.

03

Can I use my barcode scanner & printer?

Yes. RetailFlow supports USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners, plus most ESC/POS thermal printers (58mm and 80mm).

04

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, or bank transfer. Pay monthly or buy a lifetime license. We send you an activation key — no credit card needed.

GCash Maya Bank transfer
05

What happens if I cancel?

Your data stays on your device. You can export everything to CSV at any time. No lock-in.

06

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data lives on your device — we never see it. Optional encrypted local backups to USB or LAN drive. No cloud means no breach risk on our end.

07

Do you offer training and support?

Yes. Email and Messenger support on all plans. Pro and Custom get priority support and optional on-site setup (Custom only).

Round 2 — pick a fresh FAQ direction

Different from the V1–V4 set.

V5 · Editorial Open

All answers visible, magazine-style

No accordion. Each Q is a numbered "01 / 02 / 03" article-style section with the answer right below. Big ghost numerals on the left. Reads like a magazine FAQ column.

✅ Best SEO (everything indexed)
✅ Most readable / scannable
⚠️ Longer scroll for many Qs
V6 · Sticky Notes

Pinned sticky notes on a corkboard

8 colored sticky notes (saffron / forest tint / burnt) at slight rotations, each with a circular "pushpin" at top. Final note is "Got another?" in handwritten Caveat font. Subtle dot pattern bg = corkboard.

✅ Most charming/distinctive
✅ Echoes Polaroid style if you used it
⚠️ Slightly less serious tone
V7 · Deck

Horizontal swipe-through cards

8 cards with snap-scrolling on horizontal axis. Each card has icon + Q + A + "01 / 07" pagination tag. Final card is a saffron CTA "Got another? Talk to us." Prev/next buttons + dot pagination.

✅ Most kinetic / mobile-friendly
✅ Compact vertical footprint
⚠️ Off-screen Qs less SEO-weighted
V8 · Bento Mosaic

Mixed-size bento with featured Q

Big featured "Most asked" card (offline question) in saffron. Smaller cards for other Qs in white + a forest-tinted "Pay" card with chips. Final dashed-border CTA. Strong hierarchy.

✅ Strong visual hierarchy
✅ Featured Q gets star treatment
✅ Cohesive with Industries V4 / Features mosaic

My pick: V8 (Bento) — featured "Most asked" card lets you anchor the offline value prop one more time, and the mosaic style is consistent with patterns used elsewhere on the page. V6 (Sticky Notes) if you want maximum playfulness. V5 (Editorial) for the cleanest read & best SEO.
